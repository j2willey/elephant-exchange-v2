## Public wording rule (Jim, 2026-11-21)

Applies to every word a visitor or guest can see: the front page, link cards, emails, help text, and launch posts.

- The app **adds to** a party's own White Elephant swap: it makes the swap more fun, keeps everyone engaged, and moves turns along. Never suggest a party needs it, or that the swap can't run without it.
- **Phones are optional.** Guests are welcome to follow along on their phones, but nobody needs one to play. Never write "everyone needs a phone" or anything like it.
- **Keep claims modest.** Say "turns move faster" or "fewer debates", not "no more arguments" or "perfect every time".
- On screen, the Feed is **💬 Chat**. Code and docs keep the name "Feed" (see `CONTEXT.md`).
