# Elephant Exchange

A live companion for White Elephant (Yankee Swap) gift exchanges. It exists to keep big exchanges (25 or more Players) moving and fun: everyone can see the Gifts in play and follow along on their phone, so they're ready when they're called. Many Organizations can run their own branded exchanges on one shared service.

## Language

### Tenancy

**Organization**:
A group (a company, a club, a family) whose look and feel its exchanges use. Anyone signed in can create one.
_Avoid_: Tenant, account, company

**Organizer**:
The one person who created an Organization; only they can rename it or change its Theme. Being an Organizer says nothing about who may host its Games.
_Avoid_: Owner, admin

**Event**:
One gathering held by an Organization, such as "Holiday Party 2026"; for now each Event has exactly one Game.
_Avoid_: Party (as a domain term)

**Theme**:
How an exchange looks: a main color, a page color, a logo and a background image, each optional. Set by an Organizer on their Organization. A Game shows its Organization's Theme, and its Host or a Co-host may override any part of it for that Game alone. A Game's Host picks its Organization (from the ones they're the Organizer of) and can change it at any time; a Game with none, and any part left unset, shows the app's own look.
_Avoid_: Branding, skin

**Ready-made Look**:
One of the Themes the app comes with: a background image and the main and page colors that go with it (Fireside, Ugly sweater, Retro '80s and so on). Picking one sets those three parts of a Game's or an Organization's Theme in one tap; they're then ordinary Theme parts, changeable one by one.
_Avoid_: Template, preset, skin

### People

**Host**:
The person who created a Game and is the authority on every Pick in it; need not hold any position in the Organization.
_Avoid_: Admin, game admin

**Co-host**:
A person the Host has authorized to make Picks in a Game on the Host's behalf, and to moderate its Feed. The Host appoints them in the **Co-hosts panel**, picking from everyone signed in whom the Game knows (by name, never by email), or by email for someone who hasn't opened it yet (a Player the Host typed in is found by name and needs an email added; no email is sent: it counts once they sign in with it). Only the Host appoints Co-hosts. The Host and each Co-host may also play; the Host says which Player each plays as (one who joined as a Player is known already), and the consoles mark those Players, since someone else has to enter their Turns.
_Avoid_: Proxy, moderator, scorekeeper

**Player**:
A person in the swap who brought a Gift and will be called up to take a Turn; may or may not have signed in.
_Avoid_: Participant

**Guest**:
A signed-in person following a Game who did not bring a Gift and is not a Player.
_Avoid_: Spectator, viewer

**Name**:
What a person goes by in a Game, for everyone to see: a Player's name on the Roster, or the one a Guest posts as. One per person per Game; changing it changes it on their earlier Posts too. The Host or a Co-host can rename anyone, from the Roster or from one of their Posts, and **lock** the name so its owner can't change it back (for an unkind name). Names go live at once, unless the Host has switched on **Approve names** (for a tough crowd): then a name someone picks for themselves shows as "New player" or "Guest" until the Host or a Co-host approves it or renames them. Names the Host and Co-hosts give, or pick for themselves, never wait.
_Avoid_: Nickname, handle, display name

**Late Arrival**:
A Player added after the Game has started; they simply join the Players not yet called.

**Victim**:
A Player whose Gift was just stolen and who must act next, ahead of anyone newly drawn.

**On Deck**:
A Player already drawn but not yet called up; drawn as a Turn starts, so they get a heads-up before their own Turn.

### Play

**Game**:
One gift exchange, from setup through the final results. The Host may end it at any moment, even mid-Turn.

**Game Tagline**:
An optional line under the Game's name, set by the Host ("Ugly sweaters encouraged"), shown on the big screen and every phone. Not a Pitch's Tagline, which is about one Gift.

**Scoreboard**:
The big screen at the party (a TV or projector), which only watches. The Host or a Co-host switches its **Screen Mode**: **Gifts** (who's up, On Deck, every Gift with its holder and Steals, the latest Posts and a QR code), **Rules**, **QR code** (filling the screen), **Catalog** (every Gift as a card with its Photo) or, once the Game is over, **Highlights**. A new Game's starts on the QR code and switches to Gifts by itself when the Game starts.
_Avoid_: TV screen, watch screen, display

**Roster**:
The list of Players in a Game.

**Roster Mode**:
How a Game's Roster is built: **Host-entered** (only the Host and Co-hosts add Players; everyone on a phone is a Guest) or **Self sign-up** (people add themselves as Players or Guests, and the Host and Co-hosts can still add Players who have no phone).

**Draw**:
Choosing the next Player to call up, at random, from the Players not yet called. Nobody knows their place in line ahead of time.
_Avoid_: Draw number, ticket, pick order

**Gift**:
A wrapped or opened present in the Game; each opened Gift is held by exactly one Player, except a Returned Gift during the Final Round. An opened Gift has a name and may have details ("Breville, barely used"); the Host can correct either at any time, which is not a Pick and is never undone. To keep up with a fast party, a Gift may be opened with no name: it's called "Ann's gift" (by who opened it, even after it's stolen), and the consoles ask **What is it?** until the Host or a Co-host names it; phones show it as details coming.

**Leftover Gift**:
An unopened Gift still on the table once every Player holds a Gift; the Host counts them only when the Final Round begins.
_Avoid_: Extra gift, spare

**Star**:
A personal mark a phone user puts on a Gift they'd like to steal; Starred Gifts float to the top of their own list.
_Avoid_: Bookmark, favorite, wishlist

**Turn**:
Everything from a Player being called up until a new Gift is opened, including any chain of Steals in between. The number of Turns so far equals the number of Gifts opened.
_Avoid_: Round

**Pick**:
A single action within a Turn: opening a new Gift, making a Steal, or taking a Returned Gift. "Turn 42 of 50 · 95 Picks."
_Avoid_: Move, exchange, action

**Steal**:
A Pick in which a Player takes an opened Gift from its current holder, who becomes a Victim.

**No Take-Backs**:
A Victim may not steal back the Gift just taken from them on their very next Pick; once they get any Gift, the restriction ends.

**Frozen**:
A Gift that has been stolen the maximum number of times and can no longer be stolen.
_Avoid_: Locked, dead

**Final Round**:
After every Player holds a Gift, the first few Players called (how many is the Host's choice) each get one chance to steal, since they had little or nothing to steal when they went.

**Returned Gift**:
The Gift a Final Round Player puts back on the table after stealing; taking it is not a Steal.

**Skip**:
Returning a called Player who isn't present to the pool of Players not yet called, and drawing another.

**Undo**:
Reversing the most recent Pick, done only by the Host or a Co-host to correct a mistaken entry.

### Sharing

**Post**:
Something a signed-in Player or Guest shares during a Game: a few words, a Photo, or both. Every Post appears in the Game's Feed; the poster may also say which Gift it's about (or post right from the Gift, with **Add a photo**; the poster, the Host or a Co-host can also attach a Post to a Gift afterwards, or take it off), and it then shows under that Gift too: its newest Post and Photo, and a count that opens every Post about it, one at a time.
_Avoid_: Comment (as a separate thing), message

**Photo**:
A picture attached to a Post, at most one per Post; a Post may be just a Photo. Photos are deleted 60 days after the Game's last move (whether it ended or was left unfinished): a Post that was only a Photo goes with it, and one with words keeps them. Before then the Host or a Co-host can download the **Album**: every visible Photo, with who posted it and what they said.

**Feed**: (shown to people as **💬 Chat** since 2026-11-21: "feed" at a party reads as food; the code keeps the name Feed)
All of a Game's visible Posts, newest first, on phones and the big screen.
_Avoid_: Chat, blog, wall

**Reply**:
A Post that answers another visible Post in the same Game. It joins the top of the Feed like any Post (following Moderation, and hidden the same way) with a small quote of the one it answers above it: who wrote it, its first words and a thumbnail of its Photo; tapping the quote goes to the original. The original shows how many replies everyone can see. If the original is later hidden or removed, the quote says so instead.
_Avoid_: Comment, thread

**Pitch**:
A Gift's story, told by its holders: a horrible Gift gets a creative, enticing story to tempt a Steal, a loved one every reason nobody should take it. Each holder writes their own part, signed with their Player's name, while they hold the Gift; it stays when the Gift is stolen, so the story builds through the night, newest part on top. A holder may change or remove only their own part, any time before the Game ends; the Host and Co-hosts may write the holder's part for them (a Player without a phone) and change any part. A part has a **Tagline** (a bold headline, up to 80 characters), the story (up to 500) and a Photo, any of them. The current holder may **Pitch it again**: their part goes back to the top of the Feed, at most every 5 minutes (the Host can change it, or set 0 for no wait); it keeps its place in the Gift's story. Each part is a Post about the Gift, so Moderation applies (Approval, Paused, Mute, Hide) and it shows in the Feed too; it can't be moved to another Gift. Separate from the Gift's name and details, which are the Host's facts.
_Avoid_: Description (that's the Host's details), story, blurb

**Reaction**:
A friendly emoji anyone signed in puts on a visible Post: 👍 Like, ❤️ Love, 😂 Funny, 🎉 Hooray, 🔥 Fire, 😮 Wow, or the holiday ones, ⛄ Snowman and the Leg lamp (our own icon, since there's no emoji for it); never a negative one. A person may put on several and take each back. Everyone, the big screen too, sees how many of each a Post has, not who gave them. Reacting isn't posting, so it still works while posting is Paused.
_Avoid_: Like (as the general name), vote, rating

**Moderation**:
The Game's rule for Posts, which the Host or a Co-host can switch at any moment: **Open** (Posts appear at once), **Approval** (each waits for the Host or a Co-host to approve it) or **Paused** (nobody can post). In any mode the Host or a Co-host can hide a single Post, or a Post with every reply under it (**Hide with replies**); showing it again brings back what was hidden with it. They can also **Mute** whoever wrote a Post: a Muted person can't post or reply in that Game (they can still react), and muting can hide all their Posts too, which unmuting brings back.

**Feed Limits**:
Against flooding the Feed: a guest may make at most 3 Posts in any minute (replies and Pitch parts count), and pitch a Gift again at most every 5 minutes. The Host sets both in Settings; 0 means no limit. The Host and Co-hosts are never limited.

**Pending Post**:
A Post made under Approval that the Host or a Co-host hasn't approved yet; only they and its poster can see it.

**Hidden Post**:
A Post the Host or a Co-host has taken down; only they and its poster can see it, and they can show it again.

### Results

**Worst Gift Vote**:
After the Game ends, the Host or a Co-host opens the vote and later closes it. Everyone signed in (Players, Guests, Hosts) has one vote for the worst Gift, which they can move until it closes, even to the Gift they hold. When it closes, the Gift with the most votes wins (the Host picks among Gifts tied for most) and the results are revealed; its holder chooses a prize from any Leftover and Returned Gifts, in the room.
_Avoid_: Downvote, poll

**Highlights**:
The night looked back on, once the Game is over: its visible Photos, the Posts with the most Reactions, the Pitch Taglines, and a few facts (the most-stolen Gift, the Worst Gift, how many played). On the Scoreboard it's a slideshow; for everyone, the Game's highlights page lays them out to scroll through, play as a slideshow and share. Only what everyone could already see.
_Avoid_: Recap, memory book, summary

**Testimonial**:
A comment a Host or guest gave after a Game and ticked as OK to quote, with the name they chose to show. It appears on the front page only once the admin publishes it, and comes down if they change it.
_Avoid_: Review, rating

**Vote Display**:
What the big screen shows while the vote is open, switchable by the Host at any moment: **Live** (the counts, growing as votes come in), **Hidden** (no counts until the reveal) or **Parade** (every Gift scrolling past, to remind the room what's out there). Under Hidden and Parade nobody sees the counts until the vote closes.
